About StudentHub Portal

StudentHub Portal is an online platform developed for students
to access academic information, university events, notices,
student services and important announcements.


Our Mission

Our mission is to provide students with a simple and effective
platform where they can access academic resources and stay
connected with university activities.

Objectives

-> Provide Acadmic Information

-> Display University announcements

-> Share Upcoming Events

-> Improve Communication between Student and University
